I am a Ph.D. student at the [National University of Singapore](https://nus.edu.sg/). Previously, I received an M.S. in Finance (Fintech) from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) and a B.E. in Computer Science from [Southeast University](https://www.seu.edu.cn/english/main.htm). My research focuses on **LLM reasoning and alignment**, **representation learning in LLMs**, and **LLM-based recommender systems / agents**.
