Leheng Sheng

Leheng Sheng is a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at {% include ed/org.html name="National University of Singapore" url="https://nus.edu.sg/" logo="/images/nus-crest.png" w="257" h="327" logo_size="sm" %}. He works on pretraining and mid-training of large language models, and studies how they reason, how to keep them aligned, and what their internal representations can do.

He is currently a research intern at {% include ed/org.html name="Tencent Hunyuan" url="https://hunyuan.tencent.com/" logo="/images/hunyuan.svg" logo_size="md" %} (Code Pretrain), and was previously a research intern at {% include ed/org.html name="ByteDance Seed" url="https://seed.bytedance.com/" logo="/images/inline/seed.png" w="327" h="111" wordmark=true %} (Pretrain Team).

He received his M.S. in Fintech from {% include ed/org.html name="Tsinghua University" url="https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/" %} and his B.E. in Computer Science from {% include ed/org.html name="Southeast University" url="https://www.seu.edu.cn/english/main.htm" %}.

Pretraining & Midtraining

Reasoning

How large reasoning models plan their thinking budget, learn from self-evolving rubrics, and reason over long contexts with gated memory.
